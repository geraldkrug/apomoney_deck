@Pomoney

Task-Based CBDC Readiness Platform

Non-monetary participation credits for education & policy simulation.



Executive Summary

Educate and engage participants using structured task programs.
Provides behavioral insights for digital currency pilots.
Safe, non-monetary, compliant, and audit-ready.



The Problem

Central banks need user engagement insights.
Pilots often fail to test incentives safely.
Lack of structured, scalable simulation environments.



Pomoney Overview

Task Groups → Tasks → Participation Credits → Analytics Dashboard



Compliance & Safety

• No issuance of CBDC or legal tender
• No wallets, payments, or financial execution
• Participation Credits are non-monetary
• Full disclaimers and audit-ready logs



Pilot Concept

User completes task groups → Credits assigned → Data tracked off-platform → Regulator observes effects.



Data & Insights

Completion rates, drop-offs, engagement patterns. Policy simulations and anonymized dashboards.



Roadmap

Phase 1: Education & Credits (ChatGPT-safe)
Phase 2: Interoperability metadata prep
Phase 3: Off-platform pilot
Phase 4: Full CBDC pilot (regulator-approved)



Why Pomoney

Compliant, safe experimentation, behavioral insights, structured incentive modeling, minimal risk.



Next Steps
